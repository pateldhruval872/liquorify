## 10. Limitations

- **Diagnostic, not acceptance.** A passing comparison only shows that both sides could be captured; fidelity is judged from the evidence.
- **Invented data in stories, the browser tests' data on screens.** Names, counts and records differ on purpose.
- **Seeded data.** The payment methods, tax categories and rates, profiles, zones, methods, package types, roles and teammates were added to the browser-test database through the Admin API. They exist only in that database, which is local and discarded, and Spree's tests and fixtures are unchanged.
- **Test providers only.** The payment methods are Spree's test gateway, a cheque and store credit, and the rate providers Spree's own, Internal and Freight. No real provider account was used and no provider was called.
- **States reached by answering a request** show how the screen renders that answer, not that the server would send it (§2.1).
- **Settings captured, not followed.** No cart, order or provider was used, so nothing here shows a setting working at checkout or on an order (§8).
- **Spree's specs ran on SQLite, locally.** CI runs Spree's suites on PostgreSQL and MySQL, and two examples run on PostgreSQL only.
- **Reference images from one machine.** The reference images were made in the pinned image on this session's machine.
- **Not independent.** The session that built the stories also compared them and accepts them under the owner's delegation, which is not an independent review.
