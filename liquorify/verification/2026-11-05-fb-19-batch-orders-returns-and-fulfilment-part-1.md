### 5.2 SD-32: nothing sends the payment link

The proposal read in the code that nothing sends EM-C-03 and that its link might not open checkout (work item §2.7). Part 1 checked both on the running stack at `08bca2e2`, with the storefront at `ba6ddcd`:

Proposed severity High: staff cannot send a customer a link to pay for a draft order, and a link sent by any other means cannot be paid through. ### 5.3 SD-33: the logistics card's weight is not rounded

### 5.4 SD-34: the order summary counts discounts and added tax twice

Proposed severity Low: a reader checking the total against its rows finds the discount or the tax counted twice. Confirmed with the audit thread and recorded in LIQ-004's [existing defects](../LIQ-004-foundation-backlog.md#existing-defects-found-by-the-baseline). Part 1 keeps the behaviour as it is.
