**What to look at.** Each link opens the image beside this list, in the revision being read; the images are those the owner accepted at `5f1a7c21`, and the pull request's file list shows the same images. Every image was looked at in light and dark before it was committed. Stories use invented products, files and values; the product screens show the browser tests' data and Spree's sample catalog. Stories that show a whole page section, a sheet or an open list are captured at the window's size, 1280 by 800 pixels; the rest are captured at the size of what they draw.

**What the Running screen column means.**

The comparison is diagnostic: it informs the owner's review and does not replace it.
- **Seen in a capture:** the state appears in a running-screen capture, linked here, but the script did not put the story beside it. The owner can look at both.
- **Not compared; checked against its source:** no running screen showed that state with the browser-test data, or the script could not reach it there, so it was checked against the component's source only. Where a reason is known, it is given. These stories are G-53 (record §7).

**How a decision is recorded.** The owner accepts or rejects in the pull request, row by row or as a whole. An image rejected is fixed in its story, its providers or its mocks, never by changing the component ([LIQ-010 §12.1](../LIQ-010-design-system-reproduction-architecture.md#121-each-batch)).
