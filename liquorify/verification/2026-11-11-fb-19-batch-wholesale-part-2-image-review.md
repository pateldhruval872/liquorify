## Notes from the review

- **SKU combobox, Suggestions.** The story's field is 356 pixels wide at the phone width. On the running quick order page the field sits in the table's SKU column, 154 pixels wide, and the suggestion list takes the field's width, so every name and SKU is cut short and the three colours of one product cannot be told apart. That is SD-110 (Medium), found by the comparison and fixed in R5 with a phone-width story. The story itself is right for the component as written.
- **Wholesale cart view, Below the minimum.** The story shows the storefront's own nudge, "Add 4 more to unlock trade pricing", worked out from the fixed minimum in `src/lib/wholesale.ts`. It shows what the component does, which is SD-106's subject, not a story defect.
- **Wholesale gate.** The gate is a server component; its stories render each branch through the storefront's data mocks, so LIQ-009 needs no U-code for it.
