### 5.1 SD-35: a claim's card shows the amount claimed, not what was paid

Found in the after-sale captures. The claims card shows each claim's `refund_total`, labelled with the returns card's "Refund total" (`order-post-sale-cards.tsx`), which is the amount the claim asked for, not what its resolution did. A claim opened for a kettle's $44.99 and settled for $30.00 to the original payment reads $44.99; one resolved by store credit, opened with no amount, reads $0.00 although $44.99 went to store credit; and a claim resolved by a replacement, or denied, still reads $44.99 (DA-ORD-04 after-sale-settled, after-sale-store-credit-and-replacement, after-sale-withdrawn). Proposed severity Medium: staff reading the card are told a wrong amount for what the store paid out. Numbered with the audit thread and recorded in LIQ-004's [existing defects](../LIQ-004-foundation-backlog.md#existing-defects-found-by-the-baseline). Part 2 keeps the behaviour as it is; a fix decision is after the baseline, in its own pull request. It is not a security or tenant-isolation finding.

### 5.2 SD-36: a canceled fulfilment's units are never offered to ship again

Found while making the canceled fulfilment's state, and checked by `fidelity/sd-36-canceled-fulfilment.spec.ts` at `3009cc37` ([the two captures and the server's answers](evidence/2026-11-05-fb-19-batch-orders-returns-and-fulfilment-part-2/sd-36/)). Order R1023, two kettles, had its only fulfilment canceled. Its Fulfillments card then reads Canceled for that parcel and offers no Add fulfillment. The server still treats the units as its own: asked for a new fulfilment of both units, it answered 201 with an unfulfilled fulfilment, which the card then showed with Mark as fulfilled; and asked to return one of the canceled, never shipped units, it answered 201 and opened RET1008 with $44.99 to refund. Read in the code, the card offers Add fulfillment only for units no fulfilment holds, and a canceled fulfilment keeps holding its units (`packages/dashboard-core/src/lib/fulfillment-items.ts`, `unfulfilledItemRows`, counts the units of every fulfilment, canceled ones too); Spree's own spec says a new fulfilment takes canceled units (`fulfillments/create_spec.rb`, "offers the canceled units to a new fulfillment and promises the stock afresh"). Proposed severity Medium: once staff cancel a fulfilment, the dashboard gives them no way to ship those items again, and the server lets a return be opened, and refunded, for items that never left. Numbered with the audit thread and recorded in LIQ-004's existing defects. Part 2 keeps the behaviour as it is. The check leaves its order behind, canceled. It is not a security or tenant-isolation finding.

### 5.5 SD-39: routing ignores a profile's ships-from locations
