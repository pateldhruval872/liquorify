# Foundation Backlog

These are the smallest engineering tasks needed for reproducible development, relevant checks and reliable verification, in priority order. Each one traces to findings in [LIQ-003](LIQ-003-engineering-baseline.md). Implementing all 172 capabilities is not a prerequisite for anything here; most stay "not assessed" until real work activates them.

This list is a planning record. Once decision D6 picks a tracker, each item becomes a work item there and this file links to it instead of describing it ([ENG-002 §2](framework/ENG-002-documentation-system.md#2-what-belongs-where)). Nothing here is authorized for implementation until the owner says so. FB-02 was authorized on 2026-10-21, and FB-19 and FB-20 the same day ([LIQ-001 §1](LIQ-001-project-profile.md#recorded-instructions-that-shape-engineering-work)). On 2026-11-04 the owner asked for the approved Spree baseline to be completed with this backlog, the completion audit and the recorded decisions as the plan. The owner delegated the routine technical, image and fidelity acceptance of approved baseline work, and its merging; gap answers and owner decisions stay with the owner ([record](verification/2026-11-04-delegated-acceptance-and-merging.md)).
